# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

Participation must remain respectful and harassment-free regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sex characteristics, sexual identity, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other people and their work with respect.
- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect private information.
- Focus discussion on the project and its users.

Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, trolling, discriminatory language, publication of private information, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violates these standards. Report conduct issues privately to `tim@loehrning.ai`. Reports will be handled with appropriate confidentiality.
