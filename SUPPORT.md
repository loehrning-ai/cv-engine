# Support

Community-supported. Issues may not receive a response. PRs welcome but not guaranteed review. Nothing here promises a maintenance schedule, a release cadence, or a fix for any particular defect. Treat the repository as a working tool published as-is rather than as a product with a support contract behind it.

After repository publication, use its issue tracker for reproducible bugs, factual errors, and focused enhancement proposals. Include the affected route or command, expected behavior, actual behavior, operating system, Python version, and reproduction steps. Do not attach your real CV, a rendered PDF of it, or a screenshot containing your name, address, phone number, or employment history. Reproduce against `content/cv.example.yaml` and redact anything that remains; an issue tracker is public and permanent, and an attachment posted once cannot be recalled.

Use `tim@loehrning.ai` for private security reports only, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Deployment help, provider accounts and billing, custom themes, and individual CV review are outside community support.
