Your data

The JSON download covers your active profile, live documents, saved jobs, import provenance, live application-packet lineage, and provider/key hints. It excludes deleted/archived records, operational audit rows, rendered or uploaded files, and encrypted key material. See the privacy policy for the full list and the controller contact for broader access requests.

  • Storage: in hosted mode, profiles, documents, and jobs are stored in the operator's Supabase project under account-scoped row-level security. Region and backup retention are operator-selected.
  • AI: when you generate or tailor, the relevant text is sent to the selected AI provider. With your own key, generations bill to that provider account.
  • PDFs: when Cloudflare R2 is configured, rendered PDFs are stored in the operator's bucket and served via short-lived signed links; otherwise PDFs stream directly.
  • Email: sign-in links use the email provider configured by the operator.
  • Analytics: product analytics run only when the operator enables and configures PostHog.
