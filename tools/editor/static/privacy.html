2. What data we process

  • Account: when hosted account features are enabled, your email address (for sign-in), an optional display name, and your language preference.
  • Your documents: the CVs, cover letters, and saved job descriptions you create. These contain whatever you choose to put in them — typically your professional history and contact details.
  • AI key (optional): if you bring your own API key for a supported AI provider, it is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM envelope encryption) and is never shown back to you, logged, or exported — we only retain the provider name and the last 4 characters so you can recognise it.
  • Usage: a minimal record of AI generations for credit accounting in hosted mode and, only when the operator enables analytics, product-usage events.

4. The AI / bring-your-own-key data flow

AI features are explicit actions you trigger (generating a cover letter, tailoring a CV). When you do, the relevant text — the content of the CV and/or job description involved — is sent to the selected AI provider (Anthropic/Claude or Google/Gemini) to produce the result. We do not send your data to any AI provider in the background.

  • Managed credits: your first few generations run on our operator-funded provider account.
  • Your own key: after that, you add your own API key for a supported AI provider. From then on, AI calls run on your provider account and are subject to that provider's terms and data handling. Your key is decrypted only in-memory, only for the moment of the outbound call, and never written to logs.

The selected provider processes API inputs under the contract attached to the operator-funded account or your own key. Review that contract and the configured retention and training controls before enabling an AI path.

5. Data locations

Data location is deployment-specific. A local-only installation keeps the render and editor paths on the operator's machine. A hosted instance may store account data, documents, and PDFs in the regions selected for its database and object storage. An AI action transmits the relevant text to the selected provider or operator-configured endpoint. The operator must publish those actual locations and any international-transfer safeguards.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can, at any time:

  • Structured account export: download JSON from Account & data. It contains your active profile, live CVs and cover letters, saved jobs, job-import provenance, live application-packet lineage, and provider/key hints. It does not include soft-deleted or archived records, usage/credit events, PDF-artifact metadata or R2 files, application-attempt audit rows, tailor-review state, uploaded photo files, or encrypted key material. Contact the controller for an access request broader than this product export.
  • Rectification: edit your profile and documents directly in the app.
  • Erasure: request deletion from Account & data. Documents and related product views are hidden immediately. You can cancel strictly before the 30-day deadline. At D+30 the production worker makes the request irrevocable, deletes the account's Cloudflare R2 object prefix when configured, deletes its exact uploaded-photo directory, and then hard-deletes the Supabase Auth user so owned database rows cascade. If R2 or photo cleanup fails, the worker leaves the database account intact and retries; completion can therefore occur after D+30.
  • Withdraw the AI key: remove your stored provider key at any time.
  • Complaint: you may lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

7. Retention

In the default production stack, account data is kept while the account exists. A deletion request hides documents immediately and becomes eligible for the ordered external-file and active-database purge at D+30. Cleanup failures are retried rather than bypassed. Database backups, analytics events, AI-provider records, email-delivery records, and their final expiry remain deployment-specific; the operator must state those retention periods here.

8. Security

Every account's data is isolated at the database level (row-level security scoped to your account). BYO API keys are envelope-encrypted with AES-256-GCM and bound to your account identity. Secrets are never logged.
